Original bundled procedural actors
=================================

The humanoid, wolf, horse and generic bird geometry, skeleton definitions and
procedural action designs generated by crates/core/src/rig.rs and
crates/runtime/src/render/mesh_gen.rs are original project assets. They are
constructed from mathematical primitives; no downloaded models, motion capture,
Mixamo content, Blender files, or other repository artwork were used as source.
This license covers the generated meshes, skeletons and animation poses as well
as their procedural definitions. It does not license imported/user assets.
